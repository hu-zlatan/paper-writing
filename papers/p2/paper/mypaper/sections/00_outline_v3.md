- **Goal:** one paragraph a reader can extract the result from: problem, method identity, headline with its two conditions, attribution, scope limit, deployability caveat, online result. One number per claim; no route labels.
- **Content, in order:**
  1. Problem: choose a low grip **hold** force whose physical slip rate is ≤ α; friction-blind conformal baselines must cover the whole friction range.
  2. Method: after a fixed preload, a ramp-down probe reads friction from the incipient-slip onset; any force rule fed with it (a one-line Coulomb plug-in or a learned quantile model) is calibrated per disturbance stratum by conformal risk control (CRC) on the binary physical-slip loss — a marginal, in-expectation bound that, as implemented, needs simulator replays.

## 1 Introduction (≈ 450 words)
- **Goal:** Motivate slip-bounded low-force grasping, name the missing ingredient (friction read online), state the research question, and give contributions that each map to a confirmed or explicitly scoped result. Every paragraph connects to the core question: *can a short tactile probe make a distribution-free physical-slip bound cheaper in hold force, and which component carries the saving?*
- **Paragraphs:**
  1. **Why a slip bound on a low force.** Fragile objects, prosthetics, gentle grasping; humans scale grip to friction after brief contact (`johansson1984roles`). Tactile controllers react to slip or estimate friction (`dong2019maintaining`, `khamis2021real`, `boonstra2024estimate`, `niu2026synchronized`, `veiga2020grip`, `berman2025autonomous`, `willemet2024grip`) but give no distribution-free slip-rate bound.
  2. **Why conformal methods alone are not enough.** CRC certifies any force rule (`angelopoulos2022conformal`, `lei2017distribution`); without friction information the calibrated multiplier must cover the whole μ range. Hence the comparison against the *strongest* non-tactile conformal baseline, selected on a disjoint split (CSF-1 RE 0.7686 vs Mondrian-CSF 0.8294 and CertGrip-P 0.8335).
  3. **Research question** (three parts): force saving at a certified physical slip rate ≤ α; which component carries it (probe vs learned model); does calibration survive shift with only binary slip feedback.
  4. **Contributions (3 bullets, reframed):**
     - (C1) *Probe protocol + calibrated plug-in.* A grasp protocol in which a friction-uninformative preload leaves a ramp-down onset probe as the only friction channel, and per-stratum CRC on the monotonised physical binary slip loss turns a one-line Coulomb plug-in on that onset — or any probe-fed rule — into a rule with marginal expected slip ≤ α in simulation. CRC on the physical loss is used because the label rule is not exactly monotone (8 / 800).
     - (C2) *Pre-registered evaluation and force accounting.* D = 0.4168 [0.3484, 0.4778] vs the friction-blind conformal baseline; mean hold force 1.085 N lower; what this costs and buys once the fixed protocol is counted (break-even hold 13.22 s against the probe-specific phases).
     - (C3) *Attribution and limits.* Ablations and baselines that locate the saving in the probe (plug-in, shrunk plug-in, onset-only, feature chain, non-tactile prior, tuned margins, reactive controllers), an empirical check of how the certificate behaves across re-splits, strata and friction, a sub-pool where the method loses, and an online binary-feedback study under three shifts.
  5. **Status sentence (one line; details §6):** of four pre-registered core decisions one is confirmed (probe arm vs baseline); the label-validity premise and the learned-vs-plug-in decision are not, and online efficiency is inconclusive.

## 3 Problem Formulation and Method (≈ 750 words)
- **Goal:** Self-contained technical description (not a workflow); every symbol in Results is defined here. Order: *probe → force rule → calibration*, plug-in first, learned model as an option.
- **3.1 Problem (≈ 120 w).** Instance x: object, pose, disturbance stratum D1–D4. F\*(x): minimal no-slip hold force under the slip criterion (2 mm tangential, 3°, or contact loss). Choose F(x) minimising E[(F − F\*)/F\*] subject to P(slip at F(x)) ≤ α. F\*_lo / F\*_hi / F\*_mid from 11-replay log-space bisection on [0.1, 100] N; they exist only in simulation.
- **3.2 Grasp protocol and friction-onset probe (≈ 170 w).** Fixed preload F₀ = 32.7 N for every instance (encodes neither m nor μ); lift and hold; squeeze F₀ → 1.5 F₀ → F₀; **ramp-down probe** at a fixed rate; a touch-only detector on the pad shear/normal ratio stops at incipient slip, giving F_onset and μ̂ = m g / (2 F_onset). Onset noise log F_onset + σ ε, σ ∈ {0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4}; primary σ 0.2 (assumed). Then ramp to the chosen hold force F. A slip during the probe counts. **State here, once, that the protocol is fixed:** preload and squeeze are protocol constants, larger than every test instance's F\*_mid (numbers in §5.1), and the probe induces micro-slip by design; the method chooses only the hold force.
- **3.3 Force rules (≈ 170 w).** All rules are F(x; λ) = min(F_max, F_base(x) e^λ).
  - **Probe plug-in (simplest instance):** F_base = m(g + a_cmd)/(2 μ̂).
  - **Shrunk probe plug-in (new):** log μ̂_s = a + b log μ̂, (a, b) by OLS of log μ on log μ̂ on the selection split only (n_sel 800; σ 0.2: a −0.3858, b 0.7397; σ 0.4: a −0.4738, b 0.4044).
  - **Learned probe model (optional):** gradient-boosted quantile predictor; feature chain P ⊂ S (+ static touch) ⊂ full (+ squeeze) ⊂ learned probe model (+ onset); onset-only = P + onset.
  - **Non-tactile baselines:** CSF-1, F_base = m(g + a_cmd)/(2 μ̄); Mondrian-CSF; non-tactile friction prior μ̃ = clip(μ·exp(σ_p ε_p), 0.1, 3.0), σ_p ∈ {0.2, 0.4, 0.8} (oracle-side; asks how good a prior must be).
  - **Uncalibrated / tuned tactile alternatives:** probe + one uniform margin m ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0}; probe + empirically tuned per-stratum margin (smallest grid λ with calibration slip ≤ α, no (n+1) correction); reactive slip-tightening (start at c · nominal force, c ∈ {0.5, 0.75}; on each detection of r̄ > r_safe, F ← 1.25 F, ≤ 4 steps; REACT-blind r_safe = 0.8 μ_min, REACT-probe r_safe = 0.8 μ̂; slip before tightening counts).
  - The baseline is selected on the selection split before any calibration or test access.
- **3.4 Calibration on the physical binary loss (≈ 190 w).** L_i(λ) = 1{physical slip in direct replay}; monotonised L̃_i(λ) = max_{λ' ≥ λ} L_i(λ'). **CRC (primary):** per stratum λ̂ = min{λ ∈ Λ : (n R̃_n(λ) + 1)/(n + 1) ≤ α} (`angelopoulos2022conformal`) ⇒ E[slip] ≤ α for an exchangeable test instance — marginal over instances and friction, per stratum, in expectation over the calibration draw. **LTT (secondary):** per stratum, slip risk ≤ α with probability ≥ 1 − δ, δ 0.1 (`angelopoulos2025learn`, `bian2023training`); not family-wise across strata. Λ: 20-point primary grid; 41-point refined grid as sensitivity (post hoc); grid-edge rule. Why the physical loss: the label rule failed the monotonicity gate (8 / 800, CP upper 0.02115 > 0.02, 7 of 8 in D2); label-route CQR (`romano2019conformalized`) is secondary. **Deployability, stated once here and in §6:** the offline calibration needs a replay of each calibration grasp at every grid force, so it is a simulation procedure; the online tracker of §3.5 needs only one binary outcome per grasp.
- **3.5 Online calibration from binary feedback (≈ 100 w).** Score-space quantile tracking (`angelopoulos2023conformala`): q_{t+1} = q_t + η (slip_t − α), one tracker per stratum, η = 0.03412 (frozen), anti-windup at F_max; score space rather than ACI (`gibbs2021adaptive`). References: decaying step (`angelopoulos2024online`), frozen, oracle recalibration (label route). Two feedback variants: label-rule feedback with the label-route warm start (pre-registered), physical direct-replay feedback with the CRC λ̂ warm start (secondary).
- **Evidence:** `v1:h1m_analysis.json → {non_monotone,"cp_upper_98.75",by_dist}`; `v3:b1_analysis.json → ols."CSF-shrunk@0.2", ols."CSF-shrunk@0.4"`; `v3:b2_analysis.json → purpose, arms`; `v1:h3_analysis.json → eta`; `v3:b3_analysis.json → eta`; `v2:a_analysis.json → {alpha, delta}`; `v1:crc_analysis.json → {n_cal_instances, n_test_instances, draws}`.

## 6 Limitations and Discussion (≈ 300 words; each caveat stated here once in full)
- **What the evidence licenses (3 sentences):** in simulation, the friction information comes from the probe and per-stratum CRC makes the saving certifiable in a marginal sense; a one-line (shrunk) plug-in on the onset captures it, and the learned model shows no demonstrated gain. The saving is about 1.1 N of mean hold force at α 0.10 and pays back the probe's own force-time only for holds longer than ≈ 13 s. A per-stratum margin tuned on calibration data lands at the same operating point here; CRC's added value is the guarantee.
- **Limits (full statements, referenced from elsewhere):**
  1. *Simulation only; σ assumed.* One simulator, rigid contact, 5 families, modelled detector. σ 0.2 sets the effect size (D 0.6789 → 0.1401 over σ 0 → 0.4); no hardware or published-detector-error grounding was done, so where a real sensor sits on the curve is unknown.
  2. *Deployability.* Offline CRC needs replays at every grid force; the deployable route is online physical-feedback tracking (3 shifts × 20 orderings, ordering-only intervals, transient overshoot to 0.1389 / 0.1368 in S1 / S3; no physical-replay oracle).
  3. *Marginal guarantee.* Per stratum, in expectation; single strata exceed α in a majority of re-splits for the learned model (0.57 / 0.69) and the lowest-friction quintile slips at 0.2161. LTT holds per stratum at δ 0.1, not family-wise.
  4. *Validity premise.* The label monotonicity premise failed; the CRC route and the refined grid were adopted after that failure.
  5. *Fixed protocol.* Preload 32.7 N and the squeeze exceed every test instance's F\*_mid; the probe induces micro-slip on every grasp; no adaptive / low preload was run.
  6. *Friction distribution and competitors.* μ is drawn independently of all non-tactile inputs; no correlated-μ pool, no deployable vision / category prior (CSF-proxy is oracle-side); one registered reactive design, no timing data.
  7. *Where it loses.* In μ ∈ [0.5, 0.8] the friction-blind baseline is better on the label route and on the refined CRC grid, not on the primary grid; slip is unmatched and the cause is untested.
  8. *Learned model.* No matched-slip shrunk-plug-in row; the σ 0 / 0.1 learned-vs-plug-in rows have no refined-grid contrast.
  9. *Multiplicity.* Holm covers v1 secondaries only; v2 / v3 rows are unadjusted, and three v3 inferiority rows have upper bounds within 0.02 of 0 (−0.0172, −0.01539, −0.01264).
- **Future work (one line):** probe-noise measurement and online physical-feedback calibration on a real gripper with an adaptive preload.
- **Evidence:** as cited in §5; `v3:a3_holm.json → families, all`; `v3:a_analysis_08e.json → A4_A5_A6."sub:mu_only:D:full_vs_CSF-1"."CI**"`.
