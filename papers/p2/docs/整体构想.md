# 整体构想 · CertGrip（第二篇，v1，2026-11-08）

## 暂定标题
Certified-Slip Grasping: Distribution-Free Risk Control for Minimal-Force Tactile Grip Regulation

## Abstract（草稿，随研究进展更新；不含任何结果数字）
A gripper that holds an unknown object must squeeze hard enough not to let it slip, yet gently enough not to crush it.
Grip-force controllers typically multiply a nominal friction estimate by a safety factor or react to slip with
hand-tuned thresholds, and say nothing about how often the chosen force will fail on new objects. We propose
CertGrip, which turns a learned predictor of the minimal no-slip force — computed from finger-pad tactile arrays and a
brief probe squeeze — into a force command with a user-specified slip probability α, using split conformal
calibration. The guarantee is finite-sample and does not require a correct friction or noise model. When the object
distribution shifts, an online variant updates the calibration from binary slip feedback alone and keeps the long-run
slip rate at α. In simulation, ground-truth minimal forces are measured per grasp by replaying the same disturbance at
different forces, which lets us check validity and measure force excess exactly. We compare against safety-factor,
point-regression, quantile-regression, Gaussian-uncertainty and ensemble baselines across objects, disturbances and
shifts. [Results to be added after stage 14.]

## 核心想法（sushi：一个想法，做透）
1. 把“选多大握力”写成单侧预测区间问题：要的是 F* 的 (1−α) 上界，而不是 F* 的点估计。分裂共形给出有限样本、
   与模型无关的保证；预测器（触觉 → F*）可以任意，校准只需一组留出的抓取。
2. 偏移时只用“这次打滑了没有”这个二值反馈做在线自适应共形（ACI 一类），真实机器人上同样可得——这是从仿真走向
   实机的接口。

## 论文结构（RSS Stage 1，5 页正文）
1. Introduction：握力两难、现有方法没有失败率保证、贡献。
2. Related work：握力 / 打滑控制（安全系数、反射式、CBF）、风险裕度抓取综合、机器人中的共形预测、触觉对策略的价值研究。
3. Problem：抓取实例、扰动、F* 定义（单调性假设与检验）、目标 P(slip) ≤ α。
4. Method：触觉特征与试探挤压、F* 预测器、分裂共形上界 / 共形风险控制、在线自适应变体、复杂度。
5. Experiments：环境与数据划分、基线、有效性（各 α 的打滑率）、效率（握力冗余）、按组条件覆盖、偏移、触觉消融。
6. Limitations：仅仿真、MuJoCo 软接触下的打滑模型、可交换性假设、只选力不选抓取位姿。
