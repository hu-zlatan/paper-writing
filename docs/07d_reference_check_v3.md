## Novelty search

Queries used (WebSearch, standard mode):
1. `distilling greedy information gain policy tactile localization neural network`
2. `imitation learning of expected information gain touch selection object pose estimation`
3. `amortized active tactile localization learned policy information gain fast inference`

Candidates examined (2018 onwards), closest first:

| paper | identifier | evidence URL | how close |
|---|---|---|---|
| Schneider, de Farias, Calandra, Chen, Peters, "APPLE: Toward General Active Perception via Reinforcement Learning" | arXiv:2505.06182 | https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.06182 | Learns a fast tactile exploration policy that includes a tactile pose-estimation task (wrench pose with a GelSight Mini), but it trains with RL (SAC/CrossQ) rather than distilling an information-gain planner. Closest in goal, different in method. |
| Tremblay, Meger, Hogan, Dudek, "Learning active tactile perception through belief-space control" | arXiv:2312.00215 | https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.00215 | Chooses actions by sampling-based MPC in belief space over a learned world model to cut uncertainty. The planner runs online and is not distilled, and the target is physical properties (mass, height), not pose. |
| Gordon, Baraki, Bui, Posa, "Active Tactile Exploration for Rigid Body Pose and Shape Estimation" | arXiv:2510.13595 | https://arxiv.org/html/2510.13595v1 | Greedy expected-information-gain touch selection for pose and shape, optimised online at every step with CEM. No learned or distilled policy, so it is a natural "expert" or baseline rather than a competitor. |
| Keleştemur, Keil, Whitney, Platt, Padır, "Learning Bayes Filter Models for Tactile Localization" | arXiv:2011.05559 | https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.05559 | Learns the filter models for tactile localization, but the touch trajectory is fixed or random and policy learning is left as future work. Not close. |

Conclusion: **nothing close was found.** No paper from 2018 onwards turned up that distils a greedy or one-step information-gain planner into a fast learned policy for tactile or touch-based object localization or pose estimation. The nearest neighbours are an RL-trained tactile active-perception policy (APPLE, 2505.06182) and per-step EIG planners that are not amortized (Gordon et al. 2510.13595). Outside touch, Choudhury et al. (#1, #2) and Nguyen et al. (#3) do the general imitation and distillation of information-gathering or search experts. This was a limited search (3 queries), not an exhaustive survey.
