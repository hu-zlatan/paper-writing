Compare with v5 (results/v5/14_analysis.md): on test5 0-299 the student was 0.2-2.5 pp below G* in all five cells, not significant, but CIs reached -6 to -8 pp, so equivalence could not be claimed; the sub-16 check (2 cells) moved the student - G* difference by about 3 pp. All v6 decision rules (H6a/H6b/H6c, Holm, TOST at 4 pp) were pre-registered in docs/07c_amendment_v6_0.md before any v6 data, and the read-out script was committed before any E8-E12 record existed.
